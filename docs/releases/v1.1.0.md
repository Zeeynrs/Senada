## What's new

### 🫧 Liquid Glass

Give Senada a new look in **Settings → Appearance → Glass effect → Liquid Glass**:

- Rounded glass panels with soft edge refraction, luminous rims and a floating player.
- A backdrop that follows the album artwork, with soft ambient colors when nothing is playing.
- Light and dark modes, your existing accent colors, and adjustable transparency through **Glass thickness**.
- A simpler translucent material for software rendering.

The existing **Album cover** and **Transparent** glass modes are still available.

### 🎶 A queue you can shape

Choose **Play next**, move or remove upcoming songs, clear the queue, or save it as a playlist. Switch on **Autoplay** in the queue or **Settings → Audio** to keep similar music playing after the queue ends.

Senada also remembers your last song, queue, position and volume. On the next launch, the session is restored **paused**; press play when you are ready to continue.

### 🎚️ More control over your sound

Select an audio output in the devices popup, save and reuse your own equalizer presets, and open the quick audio popup from the player bar for equalizer, speed, pitch and loudness controls. Changes to streaming quality and audio output now reach the playback engine and both crossfade players.

### 🎤 Lyrics that stay in time

Use the timing controls below the lyrics to move them earlier or later in **0.5-second steps**. Each song keeps its own adjustment. Local music can also use embedded lyrics from its tags when no companion `.lrc` file is present; a companion `.lrc` still takes priority.

### 📚 More ways to manage your library

- Reorder songs and remove duplicates in your own playlists.
- Refresh imported playlists from their source while keeping artwork and match information for existing songs.
- Import and export **M3U/M3U8** playlists.
- Browse local files by **album, artist or folder**.

### 🔎 Search with suggestions and history

Find music with search suggestions and recent searches. When online search is unavailable, Senada can still return matches from your local library.

### 📊 A fuller listening recap

**Your stats** now includes top albums, listening activity by hour, current and longest listening streaks, and recent plays. Use **Share stats** to create a listening recap card, choose its background, then copy it or save it as an image. Listening statistics stay on this device.

### 📱 Queue and lyrics on your phone

The phone remote now shows the queue and lyrics and supports queue management alongside playback controls. A built-in QR generator keeps the connection code available even without the optional QR package. Enable it in **Settings → Phone remote** and connect from the same Wi-Fi.

## Fixed

- Turning shuffle off restores the original order of upcoming songs.
- Audio quality and output-device changes apply consistently during crossfade.
- Refreshing an imported playlist preserves existing artwork and track-match information.
- Liquid Glass keeps **Settings** reachable on short windows and the home-page search button visible in narrow layouts.
- A denser floating-player material keeps controls readable with software rendering.
