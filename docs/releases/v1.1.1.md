## What's new

### 💿 A streak that follows your rhythm

The **daily streak** on **Your stats** now has its own musical identity: an original vinyl emblem with an equalizer at its heart. As your streak grows, it gains glowing orbits, sound waves and highlights at **1, 7, 30, 100, 200 and 500 days**.

- **Dormant until you listen today.** The record stays dim when today's listening has not qualified, even if your streak from yesterday is still connected.
- **Lights up during playback.** Listen for **30 seconds**, or half of a shorter song, to activate it without waiting for the song to finish. Seeking and paused time do not count.
- **Your next milestone, at a glance.** The new streak card shows your current day count, evolution, personal best and progress toward the next evolution.
- **Ready for a new day.** The emblem becomes dormant at local midnight. Your connected streak remains available to continue that day; missing a full day breaks the current streak. Songs crossing midnight are counted separately for each day.
- **Kept on your device.** Qualified days survive restarts, and existing listening history is preserved. Changing the statistics period does not change your streak.

### 🎛️ A calmer player bar

**Lyrics**, **Queue**, the volume controls and **Full screen** stay directly accessible. Less frequent actions now live in **More controls (`…`)**:

- Sleep timer.
- Equalizer & Audio Effects.
- Audio devices.
- Mini player (`Ctrl+M`).

Each menu item has its own icon, with consistent alignment and spacing. When a sleep timer is running, the More controls button shows an indicator and its tooltip includes the timer status. The volume slider remains visible, and existing keyboard shortcuts still work.

## Changed

### 🛠️ Liquid Glass is temporarily disabled

Liquid Glass is being reworked and is no longer selectable in **Settings → Appearance → Glass effect**. Saved Liquid Glass preferences fall back to the standard appearance. **Album cover** and **Transparent** remain available, along with your existing color mode, accent and player-bar preferences.

## Fixed

- Player controls and transport no longer crowd one another at the minimum window width.
- Long audio-output lists scroll within the device popup instead of extending beyond the window.
- The Stats page uses a compact Share button and two columns for its totals on narrow windows.
